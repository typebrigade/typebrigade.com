# Details for Speakers

Thanks so much for becoming a **Type Brigade** speaker! Foremost, our goal is to remove any concerns you have around the event, so you can just focus on presenting. So: if you have any questions or concerns, please email [Alanna](mailto:alanna@typebrigade.com) or [Kenneth](mailto:kenneth@typebrigade.com) anytime.

You can register for [Type Brigade at meetup.com/typebrigade](http://www.meetup.com/typebrigade), if you haven’t already. Then, you’ll get an email when we publicly announce the event.

## Your talk

We have more details on the format of the talks on the [Call for Speakers](http://typebrigade.com/call-for-speakers) page. In short, you have a roughtly 25–30 minute speaking slot, followed by a 5 minutes of Q&A.

To help us promote the event, please share some details about your presentation with us—it doesn’t have to be perfect or finalised, just enough so we can announce the event.

Please email the following to [Alanna](mailto:alanna@typebrigade.com):

- **Title** A title for your talk
- **Website** Your website URL  
- **Instagram** Instagram account you would like us to tag (optional)
- **Description** A brief description of your talk & bio (100-150 words)
- **Photo** A headshot (will be turned black and white for the promos)
- **Work** 4-8 images of work you would like us to show on instagram (will be cropped to a 4:5 ratio)
- **Code of Conduct** I have read and agree to the [Code of Conduct](https://typebrigade.com/code-of-conduct) (Yes/No)

## Venue

Talks will be held at the BCIT Downtown Campus (555 Seymour) in the Tech Collider on the 2nd floor.

- Speakers and organisers are encouraged to arrive by 6:00pm  
- Attendees are admitted at 6:30pm
- The host will introduce the first talk at 7:00pm
- The event usually ends around 9:00pm, and anyone interested goes for a drink afterwards

## Slides

We should be able to accommodate any standard slide resolution, but the optimal one for the screen wall is 1920×1080. Formats we’ve used without trouble:

- PDF  
- Keynote
- Anything web based ([Slid.es](http://Slid.es), Google Docs, etc.)

We have a podium for your laptop to sit on and we don’t have to use mirrored displays, so you can use speaker notes if you’d like. If you have another format, just let us know and we’ll do our best to accommodate it. You are welcome to present using your own computer (the connection is HDMI), but please make sure to arrive with enough time for us to test your machine with the projectors. We will have some support from the BCIT IT folks and can generally solve most issues with enough time.

## Other equipment

We will also have two microphones available. A stool can also be requested if you need to sit for the presentation.

## Recording

We usually record the talks at Type Brigade and publish to our [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@typebrigade) page for free to help reach locals who can’t make it to the event and a more global audience. We have a set up that captures the talk and slides in real time during the event. You will receive the following in an email just before the event but let us know if you will be sharing things that you don’t wish to recorded and we can discuss altering the recording or not recording at all.

**Is recording and publishing your talk fine with you?**

That means:

- You (the speakers) retain all the rights to the talk, and grant us a license to record the performance and slides, distribute the recording (ex. on YouTube, our site), and potentially create lightly-edited derivative works (ex. compilation clips, event intro video, social media content)
    
- We (Type Brigade) retains the rights to the video recording we’ve made, and grant you a license to make use of and distribute the recording as if its your own (ex. on your site, make clips for your own social media, extract photos, etc.)
    

If you have any concerns about that, let me know. We want to make sure the presentation experience is as stress-free as possible and well-documented for you, so we like to be clear about what we’d like to do for video recording up front—but it’s ultimately entirely up to you!

Otherwise, we (Type Brigade) agree to that, and if you can reply with “I agree!” or similar, we look forward to recording the talk.

## Photography

We have end event photographer and can generally provide copies of photos of you up on stage after the event.

## Code of Conduct

We maintain a [Code of Conduct](http://typebrigade.com/code-of-conduct) for Type Brigade, which establishes expectations for all organisers, speakers, sponsors, and attendees. It’s a continuous work in progress, with the goal of ensuring Type Brigade is a safe and welcoming community event with clearly established anti-harassment and anti-intimidation considerations.

It’s a record based upon on the work of other events we admire. Running a safe and welcoming event is something we take very seriously, so if you have feedback or questions about the Code of Conduct or our approach in general, please let either [Kenneth](mailto:kenneth@typebrigade.com) or [Alanna](mailto:alanna@typebrigade.com) know.

## Attendees

We typically see around 75 attendees per event, but the actual number can fluctuate between 50 and 150.

Our audience is about one half professional and student graphic and interaction designers, a smaller group of aspiring type designers and letterers, and the remainder work outside of the industry but have a strong interest in typography.

If you’re interested, you can also see [our full member list](http://www.meetup.com/typebrigade/members/) to get a sense of who will be there.

## Promotion

If there’s anything you’d like us to promote at the event on your behalf (a specific blog you maintain, a product you work on, your freelance work, etc.) we’d be more than happy to do what we can. Just email [Kenneth](mailto:kenneth@typebrigade.com) with any details.

Any on-going promotion you’re willing to help us out with attendance, like emailing or sharing the event with your own followers, telling co-workers, listing your talk with us on your site, etc. is always appreciated.

## Questions?
